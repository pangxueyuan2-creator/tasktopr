# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the TaskToPR community a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or technical preference.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should communicate with empathy, assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements, give and receive constructive feedback, protect other people’s privacy and credentials, and prioritize the safety of users who may rely on this tool in production workflows.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publication of private information, credential exposure, malicious proof-of-concept code, or behavior that undermines repository security and review controls is not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns through the repository’s private security/contact channel rather than public discussion when sensitive details are involved. Maintainers may clarify expectations, remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and project users.
